# Interview Guide: Instructors and Class Representatives

**Length:** 25–35 minutes · **Format:** semi-structured · **Covers:** RQ1, RQ3, RQ4, RQ5 and hypothesis H4

These participants are the **senders** of course information. Use staff codes (S1, S2 …) for instructors; class representatives who are also students keep their P-code and are marked "class rep" in the register.

Follow the same [interviewer principles](interview-guide-students.md#interviewer-principles): specific past events, open questions, show rather than tell, no mention of Sabaq.

## Before you start

- [ ] Consent obtained ([script](consent-and-privacy.md#consent-script)).
- [ ] Code assigned; [notes template](templates/interview-notes.md) ready.

## Part A · Sending information (8 min) · RQ1, RQ4

1. Walk me through **the last time you announced an assignment or a deadline**. Where did you post it, and what did you include?
2. Which channels do you use (LMS, chat group, email, in class, through a class representative)? Why those?
3. How do you phrase submission instructions such as file names, formats and where to submit? *(Ask to see a real example; collect it as an [artifact](templates/artifact-log.md) if they agree.)*

## Part B · Feedback and follow-up (8 min) · RQ4, H4

1. After you post something, **how do you know whether students saw it**?
2. Tell me about a time students **missed or misunderstood** an announcement. What happened, and what did it cost you and them?
3. How many follow-up questions do you get about things already announced? Through which channels? *(Class representatives: roughly how many per week?)*
4. How often do submissions break the instructions (wrong name, format, channel, late)? What do you do about it?

## Part C · Setting deadlines (6 min) · RQ3

1. How do you choose a due date?
2. Do you know what deadlines **other courses** have set for the same class? How?
3. Have students ever told you a week was overloaded? What happened?

## Part D · Tools and wrap-up (5 min) · RQ5

1. Which tools help you manage announcements and submissions today? What frustrates you about them?
2. If you could change one thing about how information moves between you and students, what would it be?
3. Is there anything else I should know?

## After the interview

- [ ] Complete the notes within 24 hours; delete any recording after.
- [ ] Redact and log any collected artifacts.
