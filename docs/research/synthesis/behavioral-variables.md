# Behavioral variables

Personas are built from patterns in behavior, not from demographics. Each interviewed participant is placed on every variable below, based on what they did and said. Participants who cluster on several variables suggest a persona. The variables follow the goal-directed design method (Cooper et al., *About Face*).

## Variables

| # | Variable | One end | Other end | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | Information sources | One trusted source | Many scattered sources | How much consolidation is needed |
| V2 | Planning style | Reactive, day of | Proactive, weeks ahead | Whether the timeline or the planner leads |
