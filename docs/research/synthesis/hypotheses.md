## How verdicts are reached

| Survey verdict | Qualitative evidence | Overall verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supported | Supports | **Supported** |
| Supported | Contradicts or absent | **Partly supported:** explain the difference |
| Not supported | Supports | **Partly supported:** explain the difference |
| Not supported | Contradicts or absent | **Rejected** |
| Insufficient data | Any | **Open:** decided by qualitative evidence alone, marked as weaker |

A rejected hypothesis is a result, not a failure: the matching feature is redesigned or dropped, and the change is recorded in the [design rationale](../../design-rationale/README.md).
